## Mathematics used, not merely cited

The trained pairwise reranker optimizes logistic preference over differences `f(query, relevant) - f(query, nonrelevant)`, using lexical, semantic and window scores. Fitting and evaluation use different game sets and different query wording. Document relevance is a provisional topic label, never successful treatment.

Simulation fits `P(H)` from class counts and `P(X_j | H)` from observation counts with Laplace smoothing. Tree augmentation selects edges using training conditional mutual information `I(X_i; X_j | H)` and a maximum spanning tree. Exact equality grouping is a simpler learned dependence control. Seven binary sensors allow exact enumeration of 128 states rather than a graph service or approximate inference.

Expected information gain is `H(H | observations) - sum_x P(x | observations) H(H | observations, x)`, divided by the explicitly simulated inspection cost. No repair is executed. A high estimated posterior is not reliable under noise shift, a missing fault class or correlated evidence counted independently.

Three-valued prerequisite matching uses **compatible / incompatible / unknown**. It is a deterministic constraint check, not a safety probability or learned hardware model. The history key includes AppID, GPU, driver and Proton. Different configurations do not share attempt outcomes.

We considered semantic roles, NLI, event extraction, weak supervision, causal inference, SAT/SMT, bandits and multi-objective optimization. This iteration does not claim to implement them. Better outcomes require reviewed event/goal annotations and meaningful telemetry before optimizing interventions. A temporal or textual sequence alone cannot establish a causal effect.
