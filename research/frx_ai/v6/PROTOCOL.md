# FRX AI v6 — preregistered research protocol

Registered before v6 model fitting or displaying the reserved acceptance bodies.

Only this new laboratory may change. Preserve all tracked and untracked prior
work, including v5. No application, Steam, game, prefix, system, installer, Git
index, commit, tag or push operations. No delegation or external inference.

## Resources and stopping rule

CPU experiments, four compute threads, target <=6 GiB/process. The RTX4060 is
detected but the available Torch is CPU-only; no CUDA installation/download.
Reuse pinned v2/v5 assets read-only in a separate external environment. New
weights <=300 MiB, new cache <=2 GiB, aggregate timed fitting <=90 minutes.
Initial research budget about two hours of active work, extend only for a
concrete reproduction or unresolved evaluation defect. Practical budgets, not
cgroup limits. Stop after a useful independently measured component or a
documented plateau; do not fill a training quota.

## Hypotheses and controls

H1: clause-scoped event identity, exact catalog entity linking and explicit
speaker/quotation evidence reduce cross-game/actor attribution versus assigning
the thread identity. Compare v5's public unknown-only guard too: zero attribution
errors with zero coverage is a control, not the target.

H2: extract candidate complete action groups separately from result verification.
Compare proximity, syntactic links, a trained small structured verifier, a compact
cross-encoder and an existing small NLI model. Joint results never yield separate
individual credit. Measure candidate recall before blaming the classifier.

H3: verify execution, game, actor, temporal order and observation independently.
Preexisting/conditional configurations differ from asserted changes. Unknown
scope need not erase a literal performed action; it forbids promoting its result
to a verified current-game/environment result.

H4: read-only incident replay should assess evidence closure, contradiction,
missing information and repeated failed attempts, not merely answer correctness.
Reuse v2 retrieval and contextual memory. New context matching must distinguish
source relevance from applicability; an AMD case can retain a NVIDIA report as
an explicitly incompatible analogy. No fabricated causal probabilities.

## Data and independence

The entire reserved v5 cohort is audit-only: no v6 training, development selection
or synthetic derivative of its comments. Previously published old training data
may supply provisional event/relation labels; absent labels are not automatically
negative. Audit ambiguous, joint, suggested, quoted and multi-context cases.

New development: up to eight units from each of six new game discussions.
New acceptance: up to twelve units from each of six other new game discussions.
Reserve identities and selection rules now; do not display acceptance bodies
until inference code/models/thresholds are frozen. Exclude previous source AppIDs,
URLs and duplicate body/unit hashes across all splits. A discussion can mention
a known game: score explicit event identity separately, do not claim all named
entities are unseen. Training synthetics are marked and never final-derived.

Per game, acceptance strata: six action/result, two explicit multi-context,
two questions/instructions and two hash-selected residual controls. Development:
four action/result, two explicit multi-context, one request and one residual.
One authored paragraph/comment, 40–1300 Unicode characters, at most first100
comments. Preserve full source hashes, offsets, original author metadata and
published/updated dates externally. Page title/AppID is provenance, not event
identity. Verified catalog aliases require exact matches; never infer IDs from
fuzzy name similarity. Unknown game/actor/environment stays null.

All labels remain research-agent provisional, not independent human truth.
Annotate all observed outcomes and exact complete owner sets before predictions.
Quotation/third-party reports are not the current author's executed operation.
Community attribution and temporal order are not scientific causality. A source
hash is integrity evidence, not authentication or safety approval.

Pre-fitting amendment: add a separately reported identity challenge of up to24
additional acceptance units (at most4/thread), disjoint comment IDs from the
main cohort, selected mechanically for an exact official-catalog game-name
mention. It enriches a rare phenomenon without pretending to estimate its
population frequency. Reserve before development fitting; bodies remain hidden
until the same final freeze. Official game metadata/IDs are public taxonomy,
not labels derived from v5 acceptance comments.
Exact catalog mentions yielded only two additional units. Before any fitting,
reserve up to22 further disjoint acceptance comments selected for explicit
other-game, third-party or quotation cues. Keep this context challenge separate
from the main cohort and the two exact-name units; do not conceal enrichment.
Literal game names without a verified catalog link may be retained as names,
but their AppID remains unknown.

## Metrics and selection

Strict trigger character-IoU>=0.5 one-to-one matching; performed precision,
recall/F1 and false performed; status/direction; game and actor attribution
precision/coverage with unknowns; explicit cross-game errors; complete-group
relations correct/incorrect/missed; false attributed success; negative results;
temporal and literal span integrity. Distinguish text-level reported relations
from current-environment applicability. Do not reward duplicates matching one
gold outcome multiple times. Report oracle-span controls separately.

Compare whole pipelines to frozen v5 Stanza and v5 compact on identical units.
The compact parser is a v6 candidate for CPU/no-Torch runtime cost, not chosen
because of v5's final scores. Tune only on new development. Prefer a material
reduction in dangerous errors with useful recall; reject a learned verifier with
near-zero coverage. Eligibility: at least eight supported development relation
claims, >=80% relation precision and >=25% annotated relation recall, no more
than one false success; otherwise remain experimental. Among eligible candidates
maximize relation F0.5 with explicit critical-error penalties. These are selection
rules, not population-quality guarantees. Evaluate risk/coverage curves and
calibration only with appropriate held-out labels. Bootstrap by discussion/game;
report per-game counts and limited number of clusters.

Freeze acceptance membership before final selection, then code/model/config,
then final labels before predictions. One final evaluation; no tuning afterward.
Seed Python/NumPy/Torch immediately before creating trainable weights, after
parser/tokenizer/feature extraction; record input/tensor/checkpoint hashes,
versions, predictions and paired reproduction. Inference must run offline. The
delivered primary candidate must not require Torch if a useful lightweight
alternative is demonstrated. No prediction authorizes a correction.
